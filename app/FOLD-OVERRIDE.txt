Planning-only fold override - 3 November 2026

Create or edit a routed section, choose Flowing curve / S-bend, then check
Override folds - planning only near the bottom of the editor. For a circular
corner, use Convert to flowing curve first.

You can keep the shape in a layout or named pack, edit it later, align it
with end magnets and include it in PDF/PNG pictures and parts lists. Its
dashed outline and Routed checks warning identify the planning-only piece.

The checkbox does not repair folded slots, overlapping braid or folded wood
edges. It keeps a finite visual drawing. Measurements and material quantities
for that piece are unknown and excluded from totals. Racing previews, Timing
routes and SVG/DXF construction export require repairing it and unchecking
the override. Dimensions and numerical sampling limits still apply.

The override is stored with the piece and survives pack/layout export and
library persistence. New sections start without it. Older saved pieces keep
their existing geometry. Ordinary fold validation is unchanged.
