Lane Spacing: Squeeze and Spread

Routed sections
Select a routed straight or bend in the builder and choose Lane spacing.
Enable Adjust spacing, then choose Squeeze or Spread. Enter the target spacing
and choose which lane, or the bundle centre, should stay fixed.

Choosing Spread now defaults to the innermost lane on a one-direction corner,
so the inner slot keeps its original curve and the other slots move outward.
On a straight or S-bend it defaults to the bundle centre, spreading equally
to both sides; an S-bend has no single inside lane.
Existing saved spreads retain their anchor. To change one, open Lane spacing
and click Spread outward - keep Lane N fixed. On converted circular corners
this is Lane 1; on a flowing bend it can be the last lane. Mirroring retains
the lane numbers. The button preserves your target spacing and transition.
Large changes over short bends can still create reverse curvature in moving
lanes: use a smaller spread or a longer section for a gentler transition.

Smooth midpoint / apex is the default: for example, Spread can run from
100 mm at entry to 140 mm in the middle and back to 100 mm at exit. Hold target
spacing keeps a constant middle region with adjustable transitions at each end.
Circular corners convert to editable flowing approximations as before.

FreeForm Drawing
Generate at least two lanes, select the start point of the stretch, and open
Points > Lane spacing - next stretch. Choose Squeeze or Spread, enter the
midpoint spacing and Apply spacing. The drawn lane remains fixed. Normal
spacing returns at the next point. Undo/redo and Reset this stretch are available;
Lanes > Numbering & lane spacing can reset the whole drawing.

Spread targets must be above normal spacing and at most 300 mm. Existing
squeezes retain their settings. Spacing is measured across the reference path,
not as the shortest distance between bending slots. Wider lanes can require
a larger table or gentler curve; existing geometry and clearance checks apply.

The actual slot and conductor paths drive lengths, previews, SVG/DXF and panel
exports. Saved layouts and reusable packs preserve the adjustment. Spread files
declare integration contract 1.0.0-draft.12; SlotForge Timing must support that
contract and its shared runtime before importing them.
