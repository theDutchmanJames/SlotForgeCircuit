SlotForge Circuit - changes review
3 November 2026

SCOPE
Reviewed changes after the custom-pack deletion review shipped in v0.3.20
(c793117), through v0.3.21/v0.3.22 and the pending SVG visibility update.
This includes shared branding, single-row navigation, outward corner Spread,
centred straight Spread, inspector folders/tabs/badges and CAD appearance.
Checked the associated packaging, documentation and integration changes.

FIXED
1. A removed timing piece left the collapsed Timing line & direction folder
   labelled Set. The badge now says Choose piece and shows a warning accent.
   Undo restores Set. The timing reference is retained for undo/recovery;
   there is no silent reassignment of the timing line.

2. Large SVGs could clip the new heavier black strokes at the page boundary:
   a 10 m drawing has an approximately 20 mm display stroke but previously
   only 5 mm page margins. Page margins now contain the complete stroke.
   Both appearance choices, previews and scale calculations share that frame.
   Track/table paths and physical scale remain unchanged; DXF is unaffected.

REGRESSION COVERAGE
- Timing badge: valid reference, removed piece, restored piece, unset reference.
- Large SVG stroke containment on all four edges, full size, 1:50 and compact;
  consistent frame between preview and appearance choices.
- Pending SVG appearance tests also cover identical geometry/layers/scale,
  unchanged models and rejection of unsupported appearance options.

LIMITS
Browser checks used isolated test workspaces, not the user's saved library.
No fresh Illustrator/Fusion import or native macOS/Linux installation was
performed in this review. Large or short Spread transitions can still bend
the moving lanes back; fixing the inner lane preserves that lane's path,
not a guarantee of constant curvature for every other lane.

DELIVERY
Fixes and review evidence are local. No app version, saved-data format or
integration contract change. No GitHub push or public release in this review.
