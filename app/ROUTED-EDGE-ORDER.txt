Routed edge-clearance orientation - 3 November 2026

Fixed two authoring inconsistencies:
- A new flowing sweeping-bend preset put Lane 1 outside, while the circular
  profile preview puts Lane 1 inside. Authoring presets now use the circular
  lane/clearance convention. With 50/200 mm clearances, a new sweeping bend
  has 50 mm inside and 200 mm outside. S-bends keep their lane-side clearances
  as the geometric inside changes sides.
- Straight previews ignored the saved reflection. They now show the same
  edge-clearance orientation as the placed/saved straight.

New straights and unsqueezed straight flowing transitions align their verges
when automatically joined during addition. Reflection is changed only when
it improves the match to the target surface edges and slots still match.
Equal-clearance ties retain the requested orientation. Corners are never
automatically reflected by this rule. Manual mirror and drag remain available.
Different profiles can still produce real edge steps; Routed checks report them.

Built-in routed catalogue thumbnails, editor presets and added placements
share the same orientation. Switching between straight and circular shapes
preserves which side receives the first/last clearance. Saved pack orientation
is retained when placing a catalogue section.

Existing definitions, control points, placement reflections and saved packs
are not rewritten. The low-level spline preset generator is unchanged.
To deliberately replace an old flowing shape, choose its starting shape and
Use shape; this resets its handles. Check the preview before updating a layout.

Validation: 445 app/installer tests passed. Three new regressions cover
asymmetric preset order, legacy geometry preservation, pack reflection,
straight/transition joins at both ports with mirrored and rotated targets,
equal-margin ties and preservation of corner handedness. Isolated Edge checks
reproduced the user's four-lane 90 mm spacing, 50/200 mm clearance profile,
including preview, shape switching, Use shape, saving, adding and reopening.
No layout-format, contract, app-version or public-release change.
