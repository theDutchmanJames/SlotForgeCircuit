TABLE DESIGNER AND PANEL EXPORTS

Open Table / Space guide in Track Builder, choose Base shape > Table Designer
and open the designer. The same controls are available in FreeForm > Table.
Existing rectangles and L-shaped guides remain supported. Converting an
L shape seeds rectangular boards covering the same surface.

Add boards, edit their dimensions and positions, duplicate or rotate 90 degrees.
Drag to arrange them. Magnetic edges also align board corners; hold Shift to
bypass snapping. Positions are relative to the table's upper-left origin.
Boards cannot extend left/up of that origin in this first iteration; move the
whole guide in the builder if more room is needed on those sides.

Lock an individual board to prevent dimension changes, movement or splitting.
Lock the whole guide in the builder to prevent the assembly being moved.
The displayed track is a reference overlay and is never moved by this editor.
Use Undo in the designer, or Cancel to discard the entire designer session.
Use this table returns the arrangement to the table settings; Save guide
(or Apply construction in FreeForm) commits it to the design.

SPLITTING
Select a board, choose a vertical or horizontal cut, and enter its distance
from the board's left or top edge. The dashed line previews the proposed cut.
Split selected board replaces it with two rectangular panels. Each panel must
be at least 10 mm wide. Their shared edge remains exact: no kerf is removed.
Use Splits & stock sheets to save your sheet size, edge margin, panel gap,
rotation permission and transport limits.
Overlapping boards are highlighted and must be resolved before applying.
Separate boards and openings are allowed; the outer dimensions alone do not
describe the available surface. Fit checks use the actual union of boards.

SPLIT SUGGESTIONS AND STOCK SHEETS
Open Splits & stock sheets from the Table Designer toolbar.
- Enter sheet length/width, edge margin and the gap between finished panels.
- Set the maximum transport dimensions. A panel may be turned for transport;
  the separate rotation checkbox controls 90-degree turns on stock sheets.
- Preview suggested splits divides each oversized, unlocked board into an
  even rectangular grid. Table & seams shows the proposed boundaries over
  your track. Locked boards stay intact and report why they cannot fit.
- Arrange current panels leaves existing panels intact and estimates the
  sheets needed. Stock sheets shows numbered panels and their orientation.
- Apply splits & sheet settings commits the proposal; Use sheet settings
  saves settings without changing panel boundaries. Undo restores the previous
  boards and settings together. Cancel leaves the table unchanged.

Nothing is applied until you confirm. Changing a setting disables Apply until
its preview is updated. The planner tries several rectangle-packing orders;
its estimate is deterministic but is not guaranteed to use the fewest sheets.
It does not optimize seam placement around the track. Review those joins.
The gap reserves space between finished-size panels. It is not automatic
saw kerf or cutter compensation and does not shrink any panel or slot path.
One stock sheet size is supported per plan. All panels must fit both the
usable sheet and transport limits before the plan can be applied.

EXPORTING
Save the table first. Open SVG / DXF from the builder or Import & Export.
Choose a panel in Export scope to preview and download it separately, or use
Download all panels - ZIP. Choose the slot and conductor layers you need. If sheet settings are saved,
Include stock-sheet layouts adds their full-size drawings and PDF pages.
Uncheck it to export individual panels without a stock plan.

The ZIP contains:
  panel-001.svg / .dxf, etc. - full-size millimetres; board outline plus clipped
    selected routed geometry. Circular arcs retain analytic arc geometry;
    flowing / FreeForm offsets retain their existing sampled precision.
  assembly.pdf - overview and one fitted-to-page drawing per numbered panel.
  assembly.json - panel names, dimensions and original assembly coordinates.
  READ-ME.txt - origin conventions, geometry notes and manufacturing limits.
  sheet-001.svg / .dxf, etc. (optional) - panels arranged onto stock sheets,
    with their clipped track geometry and exact finished outlines.
The PDF includes numbered stock-sheet diagrams when requested. assembly.json
also records each stock placement, panel number and clockwise rotation.
Sheet geometry is translated/rotated only, never scaled or mirrored.

SVG panel coordinates start at the panel's top-left, X right / Y down.
DXF uses the same origin with Y negated (CAD Y up). Translation to panel-local
coordinates does not scale geometry. Individual SVG exports may use the
existing explicit page scales; the all-panels ZIP always uses 1:1.
The panel outline is always included. The scale-check bar is for whole-layout
exports only. Slots and braid/tape paths are genuinely trimmed to boundaries,
not hidden behind a clip mask. Paths outside all boards are excluded.

CURRENT LIMITS
Rectangular boards and horizontal / vertical splits only. Board joins are
also the transport panel boundaries. Arbitrary polygon cuts, independently
defined transport seams, mixed stock sizes/materials, manual sheet placement,
mounting holes and alignment marks remain future work. Suggested seams are
size-based; they do not assess slot crossing angles or structural strength.

Panel CAD supports flat routed layouts, including FreeForm. A design with
non-zero heights is rejected rather than projecting an upper bridge onto the
lower board. Explicit board / deck assignment is needed for that workflow.
Whole-layout CAD still offers its existing, labelled XY projection.

PDF diagrams are assembly references, not full-size paper cutting templates.
No kerf, cutter compensation, machining depths or toolpaths are applied.
Check seam placement, conductor connections and mechanical construction before
manufacture. A slot running along a panel edge produces an export note.

DATA AND COMPATIBILITY
Up to 128 boards; total extent up to 50,000 x 50,000 mm. The arrangement is
stored with optional sheet planning settings in workspace.spaceGuide.shape
and travels with track JSON, Track Library
snapshots and workspace backups. Guide / board locking and board names do not
change the physical design revision. Board geometry does; lane paths are intact.
Board tables use integration contract 1.0.0-draft.11. Layout envelope stays v1.
Older layouts keep their existing contracts. SlotForge Timing must explicitly
support draft.11 to import a design containing this new table shape.
