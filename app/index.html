    Car size

    Shared by both preview cars. Dimensions are in track millimetres and saved with this layout.

    About scale and dimensions

    Presets scale a generic 4,500 × 1,800 mm car. Actual models vary: choose Custom and enter your model’s overall length and width, including tyres. Length runs front to back. This is a visual size guide, not a collision or clearance check.

    Tune profile

    Shared by both preview cars. Saved with your layout.

    How the preview works

    Cars brake before bends and accelerate on straights. Drift turns the body around its guide point; the guide stays in the slot. The lap times below use the same speed plan.

    Lap times

    Calculated from the Tune profile above. Set its speeds to match your cars for realistic times; real laps also depend on motor, tyres, guide and driver.

    SlotForge Timing

    Routed profile

    Start from

    Individual pieces have their own wood outline. Tabletop routing places slots in one continuous board; edge clearances are checked against the table boundary. Each section can override this default.

    Edge clearances are measured from the first and last slot centres. On circular corners the first slot is the inner slot; through a flowing S-bend the inside changes sides.

      Lane spacing

      Works on straights and bends: smoothly squeezes or spreads lanes, returning to normal spacing at both ends.       Piece clearances define its wood outline. Tabletop clearances apply to the table boundary. Profile defaults affect new sections; existing sections keep their own settings.

      CUSTOM TRACK PACK

      Create a track piece

      Measure from the centre of the bend to the inner road edge, not to a slot. Each next radius adds one road width so the surfaces nest with no gap. Borders need their own space.

      Choose a brand only if your connectors physically match. Slot spacing is independent of road width. Dimensions are your measurements, not manufacturer verification.

      Matching radius family

      Use this artwork to plan a layout even when measurements are incomplete. Planning magnets align approximate road ends; they do not create guide slots or validate physical fit. PDFs include the picture and quantities.

      These dimensions guide the drawing and magnets. Use matching road widths within the same track pack. Left/right Hybrid pieces keep their own artwork and handedness.

      Add borders
