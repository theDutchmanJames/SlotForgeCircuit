Design a sectional table, prepare its panels for export, and give your lanes more room where needed.

## What changed

- **Table Designer:** assemble rectangular boards into your table shape. Set dimensions, drag and snap, duplicate, rotate, lock and undo changes. Shared seams do not count as outside table edges.
- **Transportable panels:** split boards horizontally or vertically. Preview suggested splits using stock-sheet and transport limits before applying them.
- **Panel and stock-sheet exports:** download SVG/DXF drawings with the track clipped to each panel, local panel origins, assembly PDFs and JSON metadata. Arrange panels on sheets with margins, gaps and optional rotation.
- **Lane spacing — Squeeze or Spread:** narrow or widen routed and FreeForm lanes smoothly toward the middle of a stretch, returning to normal spacing at both ends. Existing squeeze settings remain compatible.
- **Clean up custom packs:** delete one piece or select several and delete them together. Confirmation explains what will be removed; placed pieces, saved tracks and empty named packs are preserved.
- **Reliability fixes:** safer table switching and scaling, rejected-save handling, cancellation of queued exports, and protection against deleting from an outdated pack library in another tab. Failed saves remain retryable; a display error after saving no longer reports that deletion failed.

Table cutting currently uses rectangular panels and flat layouts. Stock arrangement is a planning aid, without kerf compensation or machine toolpaths. Panel exports reject raised track geometry.

## SlotForge Timing integration

The `slotforge.layout` envelope remains version 1. Board assemblies require **1.0.0-draft.11**; Lane Spread requires **1.0.0-draft.12**. Earlier designs retain their applicable contracts. The updated handoff includes the shared runtime and specifications; Timing must explicitly support these extensions. No Timing repository changes are included.

## Install or update

1. Download **SlotForge-Circuit-v0.3.20-complete.zip** and extract the entire ZIP.
2. If an installed copy is running, click **Storage: disk** in the bottom-right corner, choose **Stop local app**, then close its tab. If saving has failed, download a workspace backup first.
3. Run **Install Windows.cmd**, **Install macOS.command**, or `sh "Install Linux.sh"`, then start Circuit again. Your Track Library and custom packs are kept.

Circuit runs in your browser on Windows, macOS and Linux. The installer starts its local storage helper; this is not a native desktop-app build. Source and Timing handoff ZIPs are also available. All three archives have SHA-256 checksums and exclude personal workspaces.
