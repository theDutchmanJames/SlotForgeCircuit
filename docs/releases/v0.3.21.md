## Smarter lane spreading

- **Straights:** new spreads expand evenly from the bundle centre.
- **Corners:** new spreads keep the innermost lane on its original rounded path and move the other lanes outward. Mirrored corners retain the correct lane.
- **Existing spreads:** select the section, open **Lane spacing**, then choose **Spread outward · keep Lane … fixed**. Your target spacing and transition settings are retained.
- Manual anchor choices remain available. S-bends default to the bundle centre because they have no single inside lane. FreeForm continues to keep the drawn slot fixed.

Saved designs keep their existing settings until edited. Very large expansions over short sections can still produce reverse curvature in moving lanes; use a smaller spread or longer section for a gentler transition. The layout format and Timing integration contracts are unchanged.

## Install or update

1. Download **SlotForge-Circuit-v0.3.21-complete.zip** and extract the entire ZIP.
2. If an installed copy is running, click **Storage: disk** in the bottom-right corner, choose **Stop local app**, then close its tab. If saving has failed, download a workspace backup first.
3. Run **Install Windows.cmd**, **Install macOS.command**, or `sh "Install Linux.sh"`, then start Circuit again. Your Track Library and custom packs are kept.
