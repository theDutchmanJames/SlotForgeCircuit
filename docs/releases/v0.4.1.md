A small update: Live preview now shows a car on every lane.

## What's new

- **One car per lane:** Live preview drives a car on every lane of the layout, up to 8, instead of only the first two. A 4-lane table now shows 4 cars. Lane 1 stays red and lane 2 blue; lanes 3–8 are green, yellow, orange, purple, teal and pink.

## Verification

57 unit test files passed on Windows, macOS and Linux, and 29 interface tests clicking through the real app passed on Windows and macOS. A new interface test opens a 2-lane and a 4-lane layout and checks that each lane has its own car and colour.
