Power taps: plan where the power base and extra feed wires join the track, and check that every lane gets enough power.

## What's new

- **Power taps:** right-click a piece and choose Power base here or Power tap here. B, T1, T2 … appear on the track.
- **Checked as you build:** Circuit flags any stretch between two feeds that is too long. On plastic track that's 10 m or 15 joints; on routed wood it depends on braid or copper tape.
- **Suggested taps:** one button adds taps where they are needed, on a straight where possible.
- **Test sheet:** type the voltage at the power base and at each test point; a lane more than 1 V down is marked.
- **In the PDF report:** a page with the feeds, the test points on the track picture and the test table.
- **Also new since v0.4.1:** the new look, better updates, faster saves and the fixes from v0.5.0.

## Details

- The power taps are in the new **Power taps** section of the Track Builder inspector (the lightning bolt). The limits can be changed for each track.
- The limits are rules of thumb from slot car guides: a feed every 5–10 m and every 12–15 joints on plastic track; braid carries one feed about 30 m, copper tape needs about five times as many. The voltage test is the real check: at any point a lane should stay within 1 V of the power base while a car is running (BSCRA track standard).
- FreeForm circuits are not supported yet, and the track must be one closed loop to be checked.
- Power marks are saved with the track. SlotForge Timing opens these tracks as before and ignores the power marks; the design revision does not change when you add taps.
- Fixed: a "ResizeObserver loop" warning when the Track sheet was shown.
