SlotForge integration draft.11 - board assemblies / table panels
Local development, 2 November 2026. Layout envelope remains version 1.

Negotiate draft.11 when workspace.spaceGuide.shape.type is "boards", even
for a space guide rather than a physical table. Earlier designs keep their
existing contracts. Reject unknown contracts before normalization as before.

The existing guide fields x, y, lengthMm, widthMm, kind and locked remain.
New shape:
{
  "type": "boards",
  "version": 1,
  "boards": [
    {"id":"panel-a", "name":"Left panel", "x":0, "y":0,
     "lengthMm":1220, "widthMm":2440, "locked":false},
    {"id":"panel-b", "name":"Right panel", "x":1220, "y":0,
     "lengthMm":1220, "widthMm":2440, "locked":false}
  ]
}

Boards are axis-aligned rectangles; rotating 90 degrees exchanges dimensions.
Board x/y are relative to guide x/y, non-negative, and the entire rectangle must
fit within the guide's bounding dimensions. Board widths/lengths >= 10 mm;
guide extents <= 50,000 mm. Count 1-128; unique IDs matching [\w-]{1,100}; names
1-80 characters, nonblank, no control characters; locks strictly boolean.
Unknown shape and board fields, non-finite values and invalid sizes reject.
Overlaps can be represented for recovery, but the designer and panel exporter
require them to be resolved before completion. Disconnected boards are allowed.

The physical table is the UNION of board rectangles. Shared internal seams
are not external edges for table clearance. Missing areas and holes stay empty.
Use TableShape.contours(), path(), contains(), clearance() and fits(); the old
single-polygon helper is for rectangular / L-shaped guides only.
Copy table-boards.js alongside table-shape.js and the existing runtime modules.

Physical design revisions include board position/dimensions when kind=table.
Names, IDs and locks are editor metadata; changing those alone does not alter
the physical design revision. Track placements, route IDs, lane geometry,
heights, measured/calc length distinctions and sensor authority are unchanged.

Panel SVG/DXF exports are separate construction artifacts, not timing inputs.
They translate trimmed geometry to each panel's local origin. Current exports
are flat only; non-zero piece/anchor heights reject until deck assignment exists.
The ZIP manifest slotforge.table-panels v1 supplies original world origins,
dimensions, names and filenames. No cutter/kerf compensation is applied.

Coverage: app/table-panels.test.cjs, including validation, union geometry,
holes/disconnections, shared seams, revision stability, contract round trips,
analytic arc trimming, flat-only guard, PDF and ZIP structure.
No SlotForge Timing repository changes are included.

Optional stock planning (same unreleased draft.11)
shape.planning may contain:
{"version":1,"sheetLengthMm":2440,"sheetWidthMm":1220,"marginMm":10,
 "gapMm":6,"allowRotation":true,"transportLengthMm":2440,
 "transportWidthMm":1220}
All keys are required when planning is present; unknown keys reject. Sheet and
transport sizes are finite 10..50000 mm, margin/gap finite 0..1000 mm; the usable
sheet after two margins must have at least 10 mm on both axes. Rotation is a
strict boolean. These are editor/manufacturing settings, omitted from physical
revision hashing and never used to resize lane geometry. Existing board shapes
without planning retain their behavior. Add table-stock.js beside table-boards.js
in shared runtime distributions, including for legacy layout support.

Stock drawing exports use full-size sheet-local coordinates, X right/Y down in
SVG and Y negated in DXF. A clockwise 90-degree panel rotation transforms its
local (x,y) to (panelWidth-y,x), then applies the sheet translation. Bulges stay
unchanged before the usual DXF Y-axis reflection. No slot stretching, mirroring,
kerf compensation or manufacturing cut sequence is introduced. Stock placements
are export artifacts; they do not relocate the track on its assembled table.
