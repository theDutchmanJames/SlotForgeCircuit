SlotForge integration draft.14 - Fixed-surface plastic lane adjustments
9 November 2026. Main slotforge.layout envelope remains version 1.

Select draft.14 when any definition has parameters.laneAdjustment. This
takes precedence over draft.13; all earlier contracts remain supported.
Consumers must explicitly support this contract. Do not silently ignore the
field or substitute the original uniform slots when importing a design.

parameters.laneAdjustment = {
  version: 1,
  mode: "squeeze" | "spread",
  spacingMm: number,
  anchor: "centre" | "inner"
}

Supported on non-routed straight and circular curve roads with 2-8 lanes,
without laneChange. Straights require "centre". Curves allow "inner"
(keep the smallest-radius slot fixed) or "centre" (keep the slot bundle
centre fixed). Reflection transforms the complete definition as usual.
Normal slot offsets, dimensions and family remain in the base parameters.
The target is the average adjacent spacing for uneven explicit offsets;
their individual gaps change proportionally. It must be below normal for
Squeeze, above normal for Spread, and pass geometry validation.

For normalized centreline fraction t, use b(t)=64*t^3*(1-t)^3.
With base offset o, anchor a, mean normal spacing s and target z:
  adjustedOffset(t) = o + (o-a)*(z/s-1)*b(t)
The anchor is the first offset for "inner" and the mean of the first and
last offsets for "centre". Both ends retain their offsets, tangents and
curvature. Slots and rendered rails change; the road outline, bounds,
centreline length, connector positions, road markings and borders do not.

Use app/plastic-lanes.js (required by core.js; load before core.js in a
browser) to rebuild routes. Lane segments use the existing polyline schema
with points, distancesMm, headingsDeg, curvaturesPerMm, referenceFractions
and lengthMm. Adaptive chord sampling targets 0.02 mm. Endpoints match the
original connectors exactly. Slot lengths are calculated from the samples;
the road centreline remains the original length. Timing offsets interpolate
referenceFractions rather than treating varying slot length as uniform.

Reject malformed settings, unsupported shapes, rails outside the fixed
road, folded conductors and insufficient separation. No override exists
for this plastic adjustment. Rail envelopes follow Circuit's existing
family drawing widths: 4 mm AFX, 7 mm Carrera GO, 11 mm Fleischmann,
otherwise 8 mm. These are planning geometry, not fabrication certification.

The editor creates a new custom definition for a selected placement; other
instances keep their definitions. Route identity is unchanged when end
topology is unchanged, while design revision and lane lengths can change.
User-created variants are not manufacturer product specifications.

Pack export uses slotforge.custom-pack version 3 when any piece has this
field; custom-library uses version 4. Older non-adjusted packs retain their
previous versions. Layouts, packs and workspace backups preserve settings.

"Colour by piece" is a local view preference. It is not serialized in
layout definitions, pack definitions, exports or the integration contract.
Original piece colours and physical dimensions remain unchanged.
