Optional preview appearance metadata - 3 November 2026

workspace.previewCar is an optional per-layout visual preference:
  {"version":1,"scale":"1:32"}
  {"version":1,"scale":"custom","lengthMm":146,"widthMm":67}

Supported scales: 1:32, 1:24, 1:42, 1:64, custom. Presets divide a generic
4,500 x 1,800 mm reference car by the denominator. Custom values are finite
millimetres from 5 through 1,000. Presets must not supply dimensional fields.
Unknown versions/scales/fields and malformed custom dimensions reject in the
updated runtime. An absent preference defaults to 1:32 in the Circuit UI;
validation does not add a field to older files.

Copy app/preview-car.js alongside core.js. In browsers, load preview-car.js
before core.js; it supplies PreviewCar. In Node it is required by core.js.
Forge.PreviewCar exposes settings(), dimensions() and draw(). draw() returns
a silhouette anchored at a guide point 10% of its length behind the nose;
render in world millimetres, then apply travel heading and visual drift about
that guide. Actual model dimensions and guide positions vary.

This field changes appearance only. It does not change route IDs, geometry,
lengths, speed/braking/drift parameters, laps or results. It is excluded from
the race-route revision just like other non-geometric workspace preferences.
No new integration contract or layout-envelope version is required. Receivers
may ignore it and use their own car artwork/size. Preserve it when returning
a design to Circuit if you want its chosen appearance retained.

No SlotForge Timing repository changes are included.
